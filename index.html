Youngju Na

Ph.D. Candidate, School of Computing, KAIST

I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Scalable Graphics, Vision, and Robotics (SGVR) Lab at KAIST, advised by Sung-Eui Yoon. Last summer, I was fortunate to join the Spatial AI team at NAVER LABS as a research intern (advised by Suyong Yeon). The goal of my research is to empower AI with spatial understanding by reconstructing, generating, and interacting within 3D scenes. Recently, I’ve been focusing on world models, robot vision, motion modeling, and rendering.
